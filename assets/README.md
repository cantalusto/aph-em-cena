# Ilustração de abertura

Criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens em 12/11/2026 para a seção de abertura do APH em Cena. É uma cena ilustrativa, sem orientação de técnicas clínicas.

Arquivos publicados: `colisao-aph-640.webp` (640 × 427) e `colisao-aph-960.webp` (960 × 640). O PNG gerado foi preservado fora do repositório; as versões WebP foram apenas redimensionadas e comprimidas para a web.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original illustration for the right-hand image panel of a Brazilian first-aid practical training website hero. Create one polished, landscape 3:2 editorial illustration, premium isometric miniature / softly rendered 3D cut-paper aesthetic with crisp readable silhouettes and subtle texture. Scene: a controlled simulated road-collision training scene, two passenger cars (one warm terracotta-orange, one warm ivory) stopped nose-to-side at a modest angle on a short diagonal gray-green road, dashed cream center markings, a few small orange traffic cones establishing a safe boundary. Add a small generic emergency response van farther back with a muted amber light, no logos. No people needed. Composition: single cohesive compact vignette, collision cars central, full cars and cones visible with generous breathing room, road runs lower left to upper right, camera elevated three-quarter isometric. Background and edges deep forest green matching hex #152f29, seamless visual blend into a dark-green website card; soft dark-green shadows, restrained sage foliage accents, warm ivory and pale lime highlights. Lighting soft, calm, professional, educational, not sensational. Attractive and visibly richer than a basic SVG map, suitable for 400px wide display. This is an illustrative scenario, NOT an anatomical or procedural diagram. Do not create a screenshot, browser frame, UI, card border, rounded container, heading, button, labels, numbers, text, letters, typography, emblems, medical cross symbols, watermarks, gore, injured people, flames, smoke or dramatic devastation. Output the illustration itself, edge-to-edge dark green background.
